OyVey shows this week’s Torah portion with the Hebrew and Gregorian date, the previous and next portions, and the full Hebrew text with an optional English translation, split by aliyot.

  • Israel or outside-Israel reading schedule
  • Optional Friday reminder naming the week’s portion
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
  • Works offline with the last loaded portion; light and dark mode

Available for iPhone and iPad.

OyVey is free, with no ads and no accounts. Calendar data from Hebcal; texts from Sefaria.
