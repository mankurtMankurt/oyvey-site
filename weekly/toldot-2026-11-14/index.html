Toldot (Genesis 25:19-28:9) is read on Shabbat, 14 November 2026 (4 Kislev 5787), in Israel and outside Israel alike. The haftarah is Malachi 1:1-2:7.

Isaac and Rebekah's twins, Esau and Jacob, struggle with each other from the womb. Esau comes home famished and sells his birthright to Jacob for a pot of stew. Driven by famine to Gerar, Isaac prospers despite quarrels over wells. Years later, with Isaac old and blind, Rebekah dresses Jacob in Esau's clothes to receive the firstborn's blessing; Esau vows revenge, and Jacob is sent away to find a wife.
