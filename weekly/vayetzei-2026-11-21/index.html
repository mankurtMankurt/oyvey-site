Vayetzei (Genesis 28:10-32:3) is read on Shabbat, 21 November 2026 (11 Kislev 5787), in Israel and outside Israel alike. The Ashkenazi haftarah is Hosea 12:13-14:10; Sephardi custom reads Hosea 11:7-12:12.

Jacob leaves home, dreams of a ladder reaching to heaven at Bethel, and arrives at his uncle Laban's house. He works seven years for Rachel, is given Leah instead, and works seven more. His family grows - eleven sons and a daughter - and so does his flock, until he slips away from Laban and sets out for home.
